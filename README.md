Entry for the [One-Line Laugh Sprint](https://make.fun/bounty/u-daring-gecko-25-one-line-laugh-sprint-cda708b2) bounty on make.fun — one original funny line, at most 200 characters, on everyday life.

## The line

> My superpower is sensing the exact moment my toast burns — always three seconds after it already has.

A useless superpower: the power is real and specific, and it arrives exactly too late to be of any use. The twist lands on the last word.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`. Nothing else is needed — the program is the entry.
